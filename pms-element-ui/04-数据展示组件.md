## 常用组合示例

### 数据卡片

```vue
<el-row :gutter="20">
  <el-col :span="6" v-for="item in cardData" :key="item.id">
    <el-card shadow="hover">
      <div slot="header">
        <span>{{ item.title }}</span>
        <el-tag :type="item.type" size="small">{{ item.status }}</el-tag>
      </div>
      <div class="content">
        <el-statistic :value="item.value"></el-statistic>
      </div>
    </el-card>
  </el-col>
</el-row>
```
